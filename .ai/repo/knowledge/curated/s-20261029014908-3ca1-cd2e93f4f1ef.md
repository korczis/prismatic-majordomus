# cargo writes CACHEDIR.TAG into a target-dir and never into a build-dir apart from it, so the reaper skipped the shared build dir before asking its size; on 2026-10-29 it held 43.4GB against its 6144MB bound with 40GB idle 100-212 min and the volume fell to 21GiB; a fail-closed check written for removing a whole directory also blocked the per-unit eviction (I4340, #2674)

Observed as defect about scripts/reap-orphans in episode s-20261029014908-3ca1.

# Evidence

- `test:test/cases/3959_the_shared_build_dir_stays_under_its_bound.sh`

# Evidence

- Measured 2026-10-29 01:05Z on 641b: `du -sh ~/.asdf/installs/rust/1.90.0/build-shared/majordomus` 43G; `ls -a` shows `.rustc_info.json` and no `CACHEDIR.TAG`; `df -g /` 21 GiB free.
- `scripts/reap-orphans --targets` on master 5370ee63d19 printed `skip — carries no CACHEDIR.TAG of cargo's: not provably the build directory cargo named` for that directory.
- `mj_rust_bound_build_dir <dir> <crate> evict` (default 60 min idle, under cargo's lock) printed `evicted 408 unit(s) holding 40.0GB ... free on its volume 21908MB before, 50121MB after`.
- Fix and enforcement: commit f565f56407d (PR #2674, I4340). Case test/cases/3959_the_shared_build_dir_stays_under_its_bound.sh proves the eviction on an untagged build-dir, and case 3125 proves it is never removed whole. With the reaper of 5370ee63d19 restored, both cases FAIL.
