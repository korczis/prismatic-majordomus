# A fresh session is told only to proceed

A session starts in the primary checkout with no task and no instruction but "proceed".
Ask for the decision. It selects the ready issue that nobody holds and that unblocks the
most, names the issue it passed over because another machine's exclusive claim meets its
scope, and reads the board and the mesh in full. Take it. The claim, the worktree and the
task are created and printed. Announce them, and implement the issue in that worktree. Once
`finish` accepts the task, push and hand the head to the landing seat. Then take once more,
so the claim is given back.

The same session is then restarted and told "proceed" again before it finished. The take
finds its record in the worktree, answers `found` for every step and creates nothing, and
the work continues where it stopped.
