# a commit of a path outside the active task's scope passes pre-commit silently: the hook runs doctor, worktree guard, pages current and providers-check and never compares the staged paths with the task's scope, so only check, finish and the pre-push catch it, after more work has been stacked on it; neighbour of I4573 (the advisory for a commit with no task at all); reported from OSCILLA's session

Observed as defect about .githooks/pre-commit in episode s-20261111110145-5143.

- `file:.githooks/pre-commit`

# Evidence

- .githooks/pre-commit at origin/master d34f159439f runs `doctor` (report only), `worktree guard`, `scripts/pages current` and `scripts/ci/providers-check --permissions-only`; none of them reads the active task's scope or the staged paths, so a commit outside the scope passes.
- The task's scope is enforced at `check`, at `finish` and in the pre-push hook (CLAUDE.md of this repository: "what refuses a change is the task's own scope, at check, at finish and in the pre-push hook").
- I4573 adds the advisory finding for a commit with no task at all; this is its neighbour, a commit with a task but outside its scope.
