## Model, effort and cost

Capability is a dial with a price. This area covers spending the wrong amount in either
direction — maximum effort on a rename, a cheap model on an architectural decision — and
the fact that the choice is normally made implicitly and recorded nowhere, so the spend
cannot be tied to an accepted outcome.
