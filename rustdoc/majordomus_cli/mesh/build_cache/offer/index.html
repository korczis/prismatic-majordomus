majordomus_cli::mesh::build_cache

Module offer 

Source
Expand description

A build-cache offer as the mesh carries it: a signed, expiring record of the cooperation journal (ADR 0157 decision 2, I2671).

Published while the store answers. A node that serves a store declares it in its node file (super::NodeFile). On every heartbeat, before it beats, its runtime probes the store and compares what it would offer now with what it last published: a healthy store not offered yet, or offered differently, is published as EventBody::BuildCacheOffered; a store whose probe stopped passing, or that is no longer declared, is withdrawn with EventBody::BuildCacheWithdrawn. Nothing is written while nothing changed, so a provider does not grow the journal by an event a heartbeat.

Signed by its origin. The record is an event of the journal: its node’s Ed25519 key signs its stream, sequence, repository and body, and a relay forwards it without being able to alter or re-attribute it. read verifies the signature again rather than trusting the road the event took.

Trusted when it is read. An offer is read only from a key the trust in force trusts: presence on a port is never trust, and a key withdrawn from the allowlist stops being read at once, although its events stay in the journal until the next restart (rule project.mesh-is-observation-not-authority).

Expiring with its runtime’s beat. The record states its lifetime, and the offer is fresh while its stream’s signed beat rose within that lifetime on the reader’s own monotonic clock: the liveness the journal already keeps for claims, no wall clock compared. A runtime that stops stops beating, and its offer is stale within its lifetime with nobody withdrawing it; and because the runtime probes its store before it beats, a beat is also its word that the store answered.

§Example

use majordomus_cli::mesh::build_cache::offer::read;
use std::time::Duration;
// a journal without an offer offers nothing, and that is a normal answer
let reading = read(&[], &|_| true, &|_| None, Duration::from_secs(30));
assert!(reading.offers.is_empty() && reading.ignored.is_empty());
