Typed coordination records in the mesh journal (I4665).

The owner decided on 2026-11-20 that the typed records agents coordinate through live in the mesh journal, the one coordination mechanism, and not in a second blackboard beside it. A record is one event kind, record_written, carried, signed and replicated like a claim or a handover; everything else here is the fold that reads it and the admission that refuses a write before it is signed.

What a record is:

  • Typed: a RecordKind from a closed set — observation, hypothesis, plan, decision, blocker, handoff, outcome — about one task (a task, intent or issue identifier) and a scope of repository paths.
  • Authored by the journal: its author is the <stream>/<session> key of the event that created it. The stream is the signature’s, so no caller can name another runtime; within a runtime, a session over MCP writes for itself alone.
  • Versioned: each write carries a revision. Only the author’s session revises its record, and only at the revision it read (compare-and-swap); another session supersedes it with a new record that links back, and never overwrites it. A runtime that restarts is a new stream: its earlier records are superseded, not revised.
  • Evidenced or asserted: the evidence state is derived, never written — a record is an assertion unless one of its sources is retrievable (a file at a commit, or a tool’s observed result).
  • Leased: a record with an expiry is stale after it; a record whose author’s session closed or whose runtime stopped beating is stale too. Stale records are flagged, never deleted: the journal is append-only, and every revision stays in the record’s history.

An older runtime relays record_written opaquely, as it relays every kind it does not know, so replication stays compatible; it simply does not fold the records.

The lifecycle, on one journal: a record written, revised by its author, superseded by another session, and folded with its history kept.

use std::sync::Arc;
use majordomus_cli::mesh::identity::NodeIdentity;
use majordomus_cli::mesh::journal::{EventBody, Journal, StreamLiveness};
use majordomus_cli::mesh::records::{RecordBody, RecordKind};
use majordomus_cli::mesh::state::fold;

let j = Journal::open(Arc::new(NodeIdentity::ephemeral().unwrap()), "0000000000000001",
    "repo".into(), None).unwrap();
let plan = RecordBody::new("r-1", "s1", RecordKind::Plan, "I1", &["docs"], "write it");
j.append_own(EventBody::RecordWritten { record: plan.clone() }).unwrap();
let revised = RecordBody { revision: 2, title: "write it twice".into(), ..plan };
j.append_own(EventBody::RecordWritten { record: revised }).unwrap();
let key = format!("{}/r-1", j.own_stream());
let mut other = RecordBody::new("r-2", "s2", RecordKind::Plan, "I1", &["docs"], "no");
other.supersedes = Some(key.clone());
j.append_own(EventBody::RecordWritten { record: other }).unwrap();

let state = fold(&j.events(), &|_| StreamLiveness::Own);
let first = state.records.iter().find(|r| r.key == key).unwrap();
assert_eq!((first.revision, first.history.len()), (2, 2), "every revision kept");
assert_eq!(first.superseded_by.len(), 1, "superseded, never overwritten");

IgnoredRecordWrite
A record write the fold did not apply, and why: a revision by a session other than the author, out of order, after the author’s session closed, or of another kind. A peer can sign whatever its own stream says; it cannot make another session’s record say it.
RecordBody
A record as the journal carries it: one revision of it. The author is not in it — the event’s stream and the session within it are — and neither is its evidence state, which is derived from its sources.
RecordConflict
Two live plans or two live decisions of different authors for one task or over meeting scopes: reported, not refused — a supersession settles it.
RecordList
The answer of mesh.records, built by select: the records the query admits, the conflicts among them, the writes the fold ignored, and the whole fold’s digest.
RecordQuery
What a reader asks of the records. Every filter narrows; none is required, and an input it does not know is refused rather than ignored.
RecordRevision
One revision in a record’s history: what it said and where it was written. Every revision is kept, which is the record’s provenance.
RecordView
A record as every runtime folds it: its current revision, its author, its links, its standing, and every revision before.
RecordWrite
What a session asks to write: a new record, or a revision of its own. The author is never in it — the session is named apart and the stream is the runtime’s own.
RecordWriteAnswer
The answer of a record write: the record’s key, the revision now current, the event that says so, and the live records it contends with — reported, not refused.
