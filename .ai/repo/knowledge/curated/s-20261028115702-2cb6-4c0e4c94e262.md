# plan done refuses an issue whose own milestone waits on an open predecessor milestone even when every criterion holds on the trunk; recording its evidence ahead makes the later close a single plan done

Observed as friction about docs/PLANNING.md in episode s-20261028115702-2cb6.

- `file:lib/plan.sh`

# Evidence

- The refusal is by construction on origin/master dd377e11c35: `apps/majordomus-cli/src/plan.rs` lines 1177-1205 ("the gate reaches the work") give every open issue of a milestone the gate has blocked the status BLOCKED and the blocker `milestone:<id>`, whatever its own depends_on say; `transition` with `Transition::Done` (plan.rs lines 2200-2207) refuses "<id> cannot be DONE while milestone:<id> is not DONE" before it looks at the issue's evidence. The test `the_milestone_gate_blocks_the_issues_underneath_it` (plan.rs line 1791) holds the blocking.
- `plan evidence` is not gated the same way: `mj_plan_evidence` in `lib/plan.sh` (line 604) refuses only an obligation the issue does not declare, so the evidence can be recorded while the milestone waits, and the close after the predecessor lands is one `plan done`.
- docs/PLANNING.md does not say either (grep for "cannot be DONE", "predecessor", "gate has blocked" on 2026-11-10): it belongs in its section on milestone status.
