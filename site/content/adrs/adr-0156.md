## 156. A failure of the trunk's CI is answered by a post-mortem record

### Context

The trunk's CI concluded in failure six times on the morning of 2026-10-13. Every one of
those failures was explained, and none of the explanations was kept anywhere a program or
the next reader could find it: they were announcements on the peer board, pull request
bodies and commit subjects. Measured on master `e506c041a4`: no document kind holds a
failure and its cause, the only reader of the forge's workflow runs is the transient
`monitor.stream`, and `majordomus why diagnose` matches product pains against moments, not
failures of this repository's own gates.

The owner ordered that day that every CI failure has a post-mortem: kept in the repository,
satisfying the layer's schema requirements, with the root cause analysed and described and
the components referenced, part of automatic troubleshooting, of the UI, the command line
and the Rust executable, derived, tested, published and enforced, and connected to the
hooks of CI, the forge and the coding agents where that is possible.

### Decision

1. **A post-mortem is a document kind.** `post-mortem`, schema `majordomus.post-mortem/v1`,
   Markdown with a closed front matter and four required sections, under
   `.ai/repo/post-mortems/`. It is declared where every kind is — `share/kinds.yaml`, the
   sources of the layer, the publication — so the index validates it, MCP serves it as a
   resource, the Cockpit lists it and the site gives each record a page, with no code
   written for any of that.
2. **A record answers runs, and a cause is a record.** The unit is the cause. A record
   names every run it answers; one cause that fails three runs is one record, and a run
   that failed for two causes is named by two.
3. **Not knowing is a status.** `draft` with the class `undetermined` is a valid record,
   and it is the only thing a record may say about a cause nobody established. `analysed`
   requires a class, components and a signal; `resolved` requires what fixed it.
4. **One module of the executable reads the records.** `postmortem` holds the judgement the
   schema cannot express, the comparison of the trunk's failed runs with the records, the
   search of a failure's text for signals, and the draft of a record for a run. Its
   capabilities are in the one registry (ADR 0002), so the command line, HTTP, MCP and the
   UI are projections.
5. **The forge is asked in one place and never by a page.** `postmortems.owed` and
   `postmortems.draft` run `gh` the way `monitor` does; an absent or unauthenticated forge
   is `observed: false` with the reason and owes nothing. A screen draws `postmortems.list`.
6. **Troubleshooting is a search, not a model.** `postmortems.diagnose` looks for every
   record's signals in the text it is given, ignoring case, and ranks by the number that
   matched. It weighs nothing and guesses nothing.
7. **Enforcement has an offline half and a forge half.** The offline half, `postmortem
   check`, is a gate of the structure job on every plan. The forge half, `postmortem owed`,
   exits 10 while a failed run past its grace has no record; it is run by the hooks that
   have the forge — the trunk's workflow and a session's start — and not inside a gate that
   must be reproducible offline.
8. **The rule does not reach back.** Runs before 2026-10-13 owe nothing.

### Alternatives rejected

- **A class of the knowledge kind.** A knowledge record is one statement with its
  provenance. A post-mortem has runs, a cause class, components and signals that programs
  read, and four sections a gate requires; forcing them into `knowledge` would leave the
  schema unable to say any of it.
- **Issues on the forge.** They are not in the repository, are not validated, and cannot be
  searched offline by the tool that needs them when the forge is the thing that is red.
- **Deriving the record from the log by a model.** The executable invokes no model (ADR
  0032). It derives the draft — the run, the jobs, the step — and a person or a session
  writes the cause.
- **A shell gate.** New automation is a typed capability (ADR 0069).

### Consequences

- A landing that turns the trunk red owes a record as well as a fix, and the record can
  land with the fix.
- The first eight records are of 2026-10-13 and were written from the job logs; one is a
  draft.
- The forge half is enforced only where a hook runs it. Until the workflow and the session
  hooks of milestone `a-failure-is-answered-by-a-record` are on the trunk, `postmortem
  owed` is a command a session runs, and the rule's proof of it is a case over a fixture.
- A signal is hand-written text. A record whose signal is too short matches unrelated
  failures; the schema's lower bound is four characters and the review of a record is where
  a signal is made specific.
