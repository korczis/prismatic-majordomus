A prompt, a change or an inference is reconciled with the intents, issues, knowledge and evidence it affects, deterministically, under one identity on every surface

intent a-change-reconciles-what-it-affects

How far reality is from it

Majordomus already has the mechanisms. The owner wants them to keep correlating six things: what a person asks for, what the repository knows, what proves it, the tasks that carry it, what peers and advisors said about it, and how the work ended. The correlation comes from the lifecycle events that already occur. A captured prompt is matched to the existing intents before anything new is proposed, and the task that follows records the prompt it followed. What a change touches is derived from the index, with the relation that reached each record. A session's knowledge is reconciled with what is already curated before a person reviews it, and a promotion stands on evidence that resolves. Every inference a decision rests on names its model, its source and its budget, and independence is counted by source. One read of an entity names how it is known and how fresh it is, and it reads the same on the command line, HTTP, MCP and the Cockpit. Hooks only sense and trigger, and deterministic work comes first. A model output is never authority, and a private record is read only on its own machine and never reaches a published byte. The work optimises verified, completed intents per unit of latency, human effort and compute, without a second store, orchestrator or registry.
