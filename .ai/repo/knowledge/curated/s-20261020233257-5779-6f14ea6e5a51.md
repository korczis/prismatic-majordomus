# a derive started as nohup ... & from a non-interactive shell ignores SIGINT (bash sets it ignored for asynchronous children), so kill -INT left it and its cargo child running on 2026-10-20; SIGTERM ran its trap and took the tree down

Observed as friction about scripts/derive in episode s-20261020233257-5779.

# Evidence

- `file:scripts/derive`

# Evidence

- 2026-10-20 ~22:53Z: `kill -INT 86094` (the top `scripts/derive`, started by `nohup scripts/derive > log 2>&1 &`)
  and `kill -INT 44860` (its cargo child) both left the processes alive (`ps -p` after 8 s and 10 s).
- `kill -TERM 86094` ended all three within 8 s, and the log's last line was
  "==> derive: stopped by SIGTERM; taking the whole process tree down".
- bash(1), "COMMAND EXECUTION ENVIRONMENT": when job control is not active, asynchronous commands
  ignore SIGINT and SIGQUIT.
