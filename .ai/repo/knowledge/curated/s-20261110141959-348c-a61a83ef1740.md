# after an issue record was dropped between two derives, the second offload derive refused with exit 11 (the derive deleted tracked files; nothing was applied) for site/content/plan/<id>.md, a page the first derive had generated and staged and no commit held; the worktree had to unstage it before the derive would apply

- `file:apps/majordomus-cli/src/offload/run.rs`

# Evidence

- On 2026-11-10 the evolve branch chore/evolve-20261110-44b dropped one issue record (a duplicate of I4357) after a first derive had generated its plan page and the page was staged. The next `majordomus offload derive .` printed "the derive deleted tracked files; nothing was applied — apply by hand from refs/offload/<id>-derived: D site/content/plan/<id>.md" and exited 11. No commit held the page; it was only in the index.
- apps/majordomus-cli/src/offload/run.rs refuses any deletion of a path the index tracks. After `git rm --cached` of the stray page, the same derive applied and the commit went through (60fc3aae730).
