# Generating a dashboard is commoditised; consistency, continuity and evidence over a long-lived project are not

On 2026-10-13 the owner compared Majordomus with a bespoke dashboard a model built in about an hour. A screen alone does not differentiate; what does is that every panel derives from one model, stays consistent across tools and models over months, and proves what happened. Until a newcomer sees that value within minutes, the objection holds in product terms.
