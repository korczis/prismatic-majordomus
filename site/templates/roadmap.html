The order, and why it is not a preference

Each milestone depends on the ones before it, and the dependency is enforced rather than intended: a milestone whose dependencies are not accepted is BLOCKED whatever its own issues say. That is why there is no percentage anywhere on this page — a bar at 100% inside a blocked milestone would be a lie the graph can already tell you is one.
