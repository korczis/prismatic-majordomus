plan.allocate: an identifier is taken by one atomic claim (I3040, ADR 0177).

§Why a survey is not enough

scripts/case-numbers next and majordomus adr next survey this tree, the sibling worktrees, every ref and the board, and both say of their answer that it is a best reading, not a reservation: a number two sessions read in the same minute is the same number. On 2026-10-17 I2795, case 3178 and case 3221 were each taken twice in one day, and the seats fell back on hand-assigned blocks of ids. What a reading cannot give, one claim can: an exclusive mesh claim on the identifier’s own key, id:<kind>/<number>, is admitted under the runtime’s admission lock (Cooperation::claim), so of two callers on one runtime exactly one holds a number and the other is told who does.

§What is surveyed

Four sources, each answering what the others cannot, and each reported with what it held: this tree, the sibling worktrees (authored and uncommitted is a real claim), every ref (every identity ever added on any branch, tag, remote or rescue ref: a renamed or deleted file leaves its number spent) and every claim the mesh has folded on id:<kind>/*, held, released or expired, so that a number reserved and given up is never handed out again while the journal remembers it. The candidate is one past the highest of them, or the caller’s at_least when that is higher (a survey the caller ran itself, the board’s announced blocks), and allocation never fills a hole. A source that could not be read refuses the allocation (source_unread): a number past what was not read may be one it holds. Allocations of one process run one at a time, because an exclusive claim does not meet another claim of its own session and only the order of the surveys keeps two allocations of one session apart.

§What it does not promise

Across linked runtimes two claims made before they sync are both admitted, and the fold then names one winner on every runtime (ADR 0067); the loser’s claim reads conflicted on mesh.state and its holder must allocate again. Fencing is mesh-journal-converges’ work, not this capability’s. The reservation lives while its session does; once the record carrying the number is pushed, every ref survey sees it and the claim may be released with majordomus mesh release <key>.

use majordomus_cli::capability::builtin::allocate::IdKind;

// the three kinds, spelled as every surface spells them
let words: Vec<_> = [IdKind::Issue, IdKind::Case, IdKind::Adr]
    .iter()
    .map(|k| serde_json::to_value(k).unwrap())
    .collect();
assert_eq!(words, ["issue", "case", "adr"]);
