# background wait loops of the form until grep; do sleep; done run by delegated agents grew to 11-12 GB footprint each in zsh and drove swap to 18-23 GB and free disk under the floor twice in one night; a bounded single-shot wait or a completion notification is the safe form

Observed as repetition about gate:liveness in episode s-20261028132518-6330.

# Evidence

- `file:lib/evidence.sh`

# Evidence

- The measurement in the record: 11-12 GB resident per `until grep; do sleep; done` loop in zsh, swap 18-23 GB, free disk under the floor twice in one night.
- On 2026-11-05 session f9c3d552 ran every wait as one background command that exits on completion (`until ! ssh host 'kill -0 <pid>'; do sleep 45; done`, then one read) and the machine's swap stayed at 7.5 GB of 9 GB (`sysctl vm.swapusage`) through a day of waits.
