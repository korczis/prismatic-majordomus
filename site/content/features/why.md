## What it does

`majordomus why` lists the moments, narrowed by any facet the catalogue reports; `why
diagnose` takes the signals a reader recognises and answers with the areas they weigh
towards and the mechanisms that answer them, each recommendation carrying the moments that
produced it; `why validate` refuses a reference that resolves to nothing, offering the
nearest candidate.

## What it does not do

A moment that would be fixed by a better model does not belong in the catalogue. There is
no weighting and no percentage in a diagnosis, because there is no model behind one.
