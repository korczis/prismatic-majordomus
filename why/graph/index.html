The map

Every moment with who recognises it, the area it falls under, and the commands, capabilities, claims, rules and use cases that answer it — derived from the moments themselves.

Read the other way it answers the coverage question: which moments does this capability answer, which does this rule govern, and which parts of operations have nothing behind them yet. Nothing here is authored — majordomus why show <id> and GET /api/v1/graph?id=why derive the same edges from the same records.
