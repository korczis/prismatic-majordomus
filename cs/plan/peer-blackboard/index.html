peer-blackboard — The peer blackboard is one projection, pushed live to every surface

One capability computes the blackboard from the records that exist; a change of the board or the journal is pushed over one WebSocket to every page that follows it, with the monitor's cursor for resume; /ui, /cockpit and the home page read the same frames; the published site carries a public-safe snapshot labelled as one.

Problém

Who is working on what is already recorded — the board of every checkout (peers.list, gathered per ADR 0044), the mesh journal (sessions, claims, reviews, handovers per ADR 0067) and the monitor's folded signals (ADR 0133) — but no surface shows it as one picture and none shows it live. /cockpit/peers renders peers.list once and never changes; /ui has no peers screen; /cockpit/monitor polls the whole monitor every five seconds; the only WebSocket, /events, carries executions and nothing else; and nothing between the board, the journal and the socket says that anything changed. The unmerged ADR 0162 draft proposed a board channel for the kiosk alone and was never implemented.

Výsledek

One capability computes the blackboard from the records that exist; a change of the board or the journal is pushed over one WebSocket to every page that follows it, with the monitor's cursor for resume; /ui, /cockpit and the home page read the same frames; the published site carries a public-safe snapshot labelled as one.

Aktuální stav

peers.list, mesh.state and monitor.stream answer separately; /cockpit/peers is static; /cockpit/monitor polls; socket.js follows executions only.

Cílový stav

blackboard.snapshot answers on every interface; a board or journal change reaches a connected page in under a second on this machine; every surface that shows peers reads that one projection.

Mimo rozsah

  • A second peer registry, a message store or a broker; a peer message kind is added to the journal only when reviews and handovers are shown not to carry what peers send each other
  • A public WebSocket into a private mesh, or credentials in a published bundle
  • Presence for a provider (OpenAI Dots included) that exposes no supported, authenticated event source

Kritéria přijetí

  • blackboard.snapshot answers the same projection over the command line, HTTP and MCP, with completeness, sources, workers, conflicts, reviews, handovers, typed relations and signals
  • A board or journal change is pushed to a connected page over one WebSocket that resumes from the monitor's cursor and resynchronises when the cursor cannot be served
  • /ui/peers, the /ui home panel, /cockpit/peers and the home page read the same frames, and say live, reconnecting, offline or snapshot truthfully
  • The published site shows a public-safe snapshot of the blackboard with its revision, and no private path, message body or scope

Validace

  • majordomus plan validate
  • bash test/run.sh 3380_the_blackboard_is_one_projection

Rizika

  • The monitor rereads the whole journal on every page; pushing it on every change multiplies that cost by the subscribers unless one computation is shared by all of them.
  • A worker's board session and its own mesh session are two records; joining them by anything weaker than an identifier invents a person.
