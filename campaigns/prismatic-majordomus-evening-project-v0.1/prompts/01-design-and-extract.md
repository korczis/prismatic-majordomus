# Prompt 01 — Design and Extract

Study locally available AI policies, workflows, prompts, session/context patterns, validation tooling, retrospectives, and operational lessons as experience. Abstract only general-purpose mechanisms. Do not copy private names, proprietary business logic, customers, internal URLs, secrets, domain-specific intelligence components, or source-specific architecture.

Think `study → abstract → challenge → synthesize`, never `copy → rename → publish`.

Design Majordomus around policy, context, durable state, explicit scope, reasoning profiles, output contracts, verification, handovers, maintenance, and termination. Keep `.majordomo/` self-contained and provider-neutral. Work in <=5-minute tasks with summary/evidence/test/replan/cleanup after each. Do not publish.
