An external brief enters as a revision with provenance, and its requirements are reconciled into the one plan

intent a-brief-is-reconciled-into-the-plan

How far reality is from it

A person hands Majordomus a brief (a prompt pack, a ZIP or a Markdown document) and it is kept as received as a campaign under campaigns/, an immutable, digest-pinned revision; a conversation enters only as workspace references read from the local workspace store, never as bytes in a tracked file; each requirement of a revision gets a content-derived identity and is reconciled into the existing intents and issues as new, existing, satisfied, partial, conflicting or superseded, the last four read from the records and the derived verdict rather than judged anew; a later revision reports only the criteria whose source requirement changed; and every intent and issue derived from it cites the segment it came from.
