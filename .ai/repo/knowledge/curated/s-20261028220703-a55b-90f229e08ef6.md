the writer resolve the key as the reader does. test/cases/111_session_per_provider.sh, new
section "by hand, inside a provider", fails on master's lib/session.sh ("a hand start inside a
provider was keyed as nobody's") and passes with the fix; cases 63, 221 and 980 still pass.
