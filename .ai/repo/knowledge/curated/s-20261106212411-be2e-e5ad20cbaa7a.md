# the #2596 trial (I4242, trial/lundra-first-wave-20261105 at a896bd86db9) routed only its coverage job to lundra and that one job filled the host twice on 2026-11-05: run 42036017630 took it from 77 to 25 GB free in 25 minutes beside the seat's derive and commit chains, and the re-dispatched run 42067809640 took it from 72 GB to 0 within 45 minutes (No space left on device broke the seat's derive and commit of the 4381 fix); the floor step at job start does not bound what the job then writes, so the coverage job owes a disk bound (a private build directory evicted at a size cap, not only at the end) before the trial can be green on a host a seat also uses

Observed as defect about .github/workflows/validate.yml in episode s-20261106212411-be2e.

- `file:.github/workflows/validate.yml`

# Evidence

- `.github/workflows/validate.yml` at 1112bace343, the coverage job (line 951), runs on `ubuntu-latest`; the trial branch `trial/lundra-first-wave-20261105` (a896bd86db9, on origin, pull request #2596, I4242) routes it to lundra, and only it: every other job of the trial went hosted.
- Run 42036017630, dispatched 13:28Z on 2026-11-05: lundra from 77 to 25 GB free in 25 minutes while the seat's derive and commit chains ran there; cancelled by the seat (its own run). Run 42067809640, the re-dispatch at 17:30Z: 72 GB to 0 within 45 minutes, "No space left on device" in the seat's derive and commit of the case 4381 fix; cancelled again.
- The job's floor step holds at its start and bounds nothing the job then writes; a coverage build's private build directory wants eviction at a size cap, not only at the end (the owner's floor is 35 GiB free before heavy work, and a coverage build adds its own tens of GB). That bound is #2596's to add before the trial can be green on a host a seat also uses.
