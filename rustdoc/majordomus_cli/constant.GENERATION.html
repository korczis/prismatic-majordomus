The generation this executable was built from: the digest generation::crate_generation takes over the crate sources, compiled in by build.rs, or unknown when the build could not read them.

VERSION says what this executable calls itself; this says what it is. Two builds of majordomus-cli 0.4.0 from different revisions of the crate carry different models and derive a repository differently, and only this tells them apart — which is why majordomus generate compares it against the tree it is asked to derive rather than comparing versions.
