description = "Each slot of machine.slot (I4006) holds one typed holder record: the holder's session key, its board peer when it has one, the purpose, the pid of the process the slot was taken for, that process's start time, taken_at, and an expected_release the taker declares. The record is written by the command that takes the slot and is never edited by hand. `majordomus machine slot handover <slot> --to <session>` moves the slot to a live session of this machine in one atomic rename. The new holder names its own pid, and the handover is a ledger line naming both sessions. The slot is given back by the command that holds it, and also when the holder is idle: none of its recorded processes and none of their descendants is a compiler or build process for longer than a policy grace (machine.slot.idle_release_seconds). Then the next reader of the slot releases it and records why. doctor reports a slot held without a live build of its holder (WARN, naming the holder, the age and the idle time), a slot past its expected_release, and a slot whose holder record does not parse. A record that does not parse is never treated as held: doctor reports it and the slot can be taken again."
