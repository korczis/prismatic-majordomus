# After an incident

A commit stood fourteen minutes behind another worktree's build lock, and the cause was
found. Evolve from it: record the observation against the rule that should have caught it,
derive and promote the lesson with the measurement as its evidence, add the shape to the
gate and the case that proves the gate refuses it, write the post-mortem as a curated note,
amend the decision whose accepted cost this was, deliver, and tell the board which builds
are bounded now and what to export until the change lands.
