{#- A terminal that replays runs that happened. Every transcript below is `site/data/generated/terminal.json`, which the generator builds by joining the lifecycle the tool declares to the use-case scenarios it executed while generating this site. The command line, the exit status and the body are the run's own; `lib/usecase.sh` redacts the machine, the clock and the identifiers out of them before they are recorded, which is why there is nothing here to sanitise a second time. Nothing in this file names a command, a count or an order. Given `tx` (a list of panels, each carrying `command`, `exit`, `output`, `use_case_title`, `route` and `assertions`), `tx_id` (a unique element id root) and `tx_label` (the tab list's accessible name), it renders what it was handed. Without JavaScript every panel renders, in order: `x-show` alone hides nothing statically, and the panel carries neither `x-cloak` nor `hidden`, which is the one combination `scripts/site-check` refuses. The tab strip is then a list of headings the reader scrolls past rather than a control that does nothing. -#}
