# Evidence

- `scripts/ci-plan` at 1112bace343, lines 457 and 459: `elif printf '%s\n' "$MATCH" | grep -q '^full'; then` and the same for `^unclassified`; `set -eu` at line 47 without pipefail, and `scripts/ci/pipefail-check` refuses a printf piped into `grep -q` only where pipefail is set, so the line reports "printf: write error: Broken pipe" and never fails the plan. `bin/majordomus`: "a here-string and one awk, never `| grep -q`: a reader that leaves at the first match closes the pipe on the writer behind it".
- This change: both lines read `grep -q '^full' <<<"$MATCH"` and `grep -q '^unclassified' <<<"$MATCH"`; `scripts/ci/pipefail-check` and `scripts/ci/shell-lint` pass and case 94 (ci-plan) passes on the changed script.
- Two pre-push runs on macbook-2 on 2026-11-05 at 14:52Z printed the message; one push exited 141 with nothing pushed, the other pushed (rc 0). Which process took the SIGPIPE in the push that failed was not identified, and this change does not claim to settle it.
