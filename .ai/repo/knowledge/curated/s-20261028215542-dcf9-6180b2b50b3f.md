# a majordomus task started in a train worktree (integration/train-10b-20261028) only to hold a reasoning record bound every push of that checkout to the pre-push finish --check for two hours, until it was closed as no_match; the session reported the check refusing the train's push on scope and mesh

# Evidence

.githooks/pre-push runs `bin/majordomus finish --check || exit $?` for every push that carries a commit, against the checkout's active task, and .ai/repo/workflows/task-lifecycle.md already said that a task whose scope is not claimed on the mesh is refused by the pre-push finish --check. The train worktree's ledger (.ai/local/state/ledger.jsonl of integration/train-10b-20261028, local) shows the task: task.started t-20261028175526-d4b2 at 17:55:27Z, profile routine, scope integration/train-10b-20261028; task.refused at 19:51:14Z (note-integrity, it had no reason yet); task.finished outcome no_match at 19:52:12Z, with the reason "Started in this worktree by mistake, only to hold a reasoning record that was then written under the I4292 task instead". For those two hours every push of the train that carried a commit ran finish --check against it. The refused push itself is what the session reported; finish --check writes no ledger event, so no record of it exists to cite. The step now lives in .ai/repo/workflows/task-lifecycle.md: start a task only in the checkout whose work it is, hold records under your own worktree's task, and close a stray one with a non-completed outcome before that checkout pushes.
