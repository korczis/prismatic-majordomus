The order, and why it is not a preference

A milestone whose dependencies are not done is BLOCKED whatever its own issues say, so it stands in later however much of its work is finished. That is why there is no percentage anywhere on this page — a bar at 100% inside a blocked milestone would be a lie the graph can already tell you is one.
