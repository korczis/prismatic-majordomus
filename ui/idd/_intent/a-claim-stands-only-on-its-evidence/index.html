What is this intent for?

What the work is for, what it holds the work to, and whether the evidence says it is met.

a-claim-stands-only-on-its-evidence

A claim that something is enforced, tested or delivered stands only on recorded evidence that would have caught it being false

plannedunknown0 of 9 criteria metClose

The owner wants every rule, guarantee and delivered feature that Majordomus states to be backed by a recorded execution at the revision being shown. For a blocking rule, that execution shows a violation being refused. Every way around a gate (an override, a disabled protection, a proposed decision cited as law) must be visible. Any statement that lacks this evidence reads as not proven on every surface, never as done.

What does it hold the work to?

  • A blocking rule is reported proven only by a recorded run of a case in which its violation is refused
  • Proof states are computed from the executions recorded for the presented revision (the CI run's own record, or the tracked ledger where that revision's run is tracked); no surface types a verdict
  • Unknown, not run and stale are never rendered as pass
  • A merge past the required check, a protection that no longer requires the verdict, and a rule resting on a proposed ADR are each reported
