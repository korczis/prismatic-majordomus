# 641b stood at 9 GiB free with 512 linked worktrees (112 GB): 73 merged and clean (18 GB), 205 merged and dirty (39 GB), 141 unmerged and clean (36 GB); removing the clean merged ones took a shell loop because majordomus worktree needs an executable the stale primary could not build

Observed as friction about docs/WORKTREES.md in episode s-20261105133225-40b8.

# Evidence

- `file:docs/WORKTREES.md`

# Evidence

- The command existed and could not be run: `majordomus worktree cleanup` ("The branches merged into the trunk whose worktree is clean or absent: what could be removed. Removes nothing without --remove") is on master since commit 1b6a7f5c360 (2026-09-30), but on a machine under its disk floor the primary's executable was stale and could not be built (ADR 0224: no entry path builds; `.githooks/pre-commit` header, I3831), so the reclaim was a shell loop of `git worktree remove`.
- Still the shape of the machine on 2026-11-10: `git worktree list | wc -l` = 492 and 12 GiB free on the data volume (`df -g`), against the 35 GiB floor.
- The remedy that needs no build, met by the evolve run of 2026-11-10: the installed release whose crate is master's runs the worktree commands when MAJORDOMUS_BIN names its executable and MAJORDOMUS_BIN_COMMIT the commit it was built from (`majordomus-cli --version` = 1.0.1, RELEASE.json commit 426e98551ff); `lib/rust_bin.sh` trusts an explicit MAJORDOMUS_BIN about freshness.
