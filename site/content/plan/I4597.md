The relation to master treats a conflict whose every conflicting hunk, on both sides, consists of lines matching the pattern as the refresh's: the executor's refresh (drain.rs refresh_branch) merges master in, runs the writer on the merged tree, stages the file it rewrote, and the derive follows. A hunk that does not match keeps the whole path authored. Each open pull request's ChangeShape records whether its change of such a path touches only matching lines, and classify::overlaps reports two pull requests that share a path only through such lines as OverlapKind::Recomputed, which the queue's contention count leaves out."
