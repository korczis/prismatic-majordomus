What an intent is, and what this page does not say

An intent is a typed record above the plan: a statement of what the work is for, the invariants it must keep, the milestones that realise it, and satisfaction criteria that each name the evidence that settles them: a test, a claim, a command or a deployment. It stores no status. Its stage follows the plan and its satisfaction follows the evidence ledger, both derived on every read.

That is why these pages show what the records and the plan decide, and not which criteria are met today. Evidence goes stale the moment a file its test names changes, so a page that printed "met" would be right on the commit it was built from and wrong on the next. The live answer is one command, majordomus intent list, the same over GET /api/v1/intents and the MCP tool majordomus_intents.

Nothing on this page is typed into the site: scripts/generate-site-data asks majordomus intent list and projects the answer; the records are under {{ it.source }}.
