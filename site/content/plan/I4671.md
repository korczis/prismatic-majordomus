description = "An enforcement entry wired into a git hook declares its cost (`cheap` or `heavy`) and, for a heavy one, `budget_seconds`. `majordomus hook run <hook>` runs a hook's declared entries in two phases. First it runs every cheap entry and the built-in preconditions: the work tree is clean apart from paths the policy declares as session state, the active task's scope is claimed and covers the change, and the finish contract holds. It refuses before any heavy entry starts if one of them fails. Then it runs the heavy entries in declared order, each under its budget. A heavy entry over its budget is stopped as a process group and reported with its elapsed time, and the policy says whether that refuses the push. An entry may declare `verdict: commit`. Its result is then recorded through `majordomus evidence --gate` under a key of the commit id, its tree, a digest of its message and a digest of the entry's own configuration. A later hook run, pre-push after commit-msg for example, reuses a recorded pass for every pushed commit whose key is unchanged and runs the entry only for the commits whose key moved. doctor reports a hook that runs a heavy entry before a cheap one, a heavy entry with no budget, and an entry wired twice into one hook."
