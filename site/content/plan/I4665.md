description = "A session writes a typed coordination record (observation, hypothesis, plan, decision, blocker, handoff, outcome) into the mesh journal as a new event kind, record_written. The record carries a task, a scope, a status, sources with their versions, an optional expiry, a revision and links to the record it supersedes. Its author is the journal's own `<stream>/<session>` key, never a name the caller supplies. Only the author's session revises a record, and only at the current revision (compare-and-swap); another session supersedes it with a new record and never overwrites it. A record's scope must lie inside a live claim of the writing session. A handoff without objective, current state, evidence, blockers and next action is refused. A retry with the same idempotency key and content is answered with the first write. The fold carries every record with its history, its derived evidence state (asserted unless a source is retrievable), its staleness (expired lease, dead or closed author) and the conflicts between live decisions and plans on overlapping scope; mesh.state, the new mesh.records and blackboard.snapshot read it."
