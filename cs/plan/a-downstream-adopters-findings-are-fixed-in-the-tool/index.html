Problém

A downstream supervised repository working on the installed 1.2.0 release (2026-11-25) met twelve generic defects in one session, each worked around by hand: advisors reported available while four of five could not answer; two copies of one credential, one stale; project rules proved only in prose, 13 blocking rules outside the doctrine registry; requirements nobody traced invisible to plan validate; one issue moved into a DONE milestone silently blocking every later READY issue; finish refusing the agent's task over a commit the owner made; an enforcement entry with args [] never recognised as wired; the versioned-hook recipe, the campsite ratchet, tracker progress comments and a mention graph all rebuilt locally; a milestone without due_on breaking a consumer; and doctor at 90 to 104 s against a 20 s budget. The repository is the evidence and is not named.
