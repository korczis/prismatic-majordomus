What a skill is, and where these come from

A skill is a provider-neutral procedure for one bounded kind of work: a directory under the repository's skills section holding SKILL.md, front matter over a body with a purpose, a procedure and an output contract. A worker loads one when the task is about what it covers, never by default.

Nothing on this page is typed into the site. The source class skill in the repository's knowledge sources discovers the files; majordomus skills list, doctor, the MCP resources majordomus://skill/<id> and this page all read that one catalogue, and majordomus skills check refuses a skill whose front matter, directory, sections or references are wrong. Adding a skill is adding its directory; every surface follows.
