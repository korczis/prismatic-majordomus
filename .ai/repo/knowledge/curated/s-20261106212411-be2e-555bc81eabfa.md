# after authoring 1.0.0 in Cargo.toml on 2026-11-06, cargo build then scripts/derive left the executable stale (older than its sources) and the commit hook and cases 10, 85 and 87b refused: mj_rust_stale (lib/rust_bin.sh) judges the executable by the mtime of apps/majordomus-cli/src, Cargo.toml and Cargo.lock against it, so whatever rewrites one of those after the build (the bump's lines in the manifest and the lock, a derive that touches the crate) makes it stale; a version change is bump, build, derive, build again, then scripts/derive-check before the commit, and the Releasing section's step 1 does not say so

Observed as friction about docs/RELEASE.md in episode s-20261106212411-be2e.

# Evidence

- `file:lib/rust_bin.sh`

# Evidence

- `lib/rust_bin.sh`, `mj_rust_stale` (lines 123-135): the executable is stale when `find apps/majordomus-cli/src Cargo.toml Cargo.lock -newer <executable>` finds anything, and never when `MAJORDOMUS_BIN` is set (line 127). `scripts/derive` lines 214-230: with `MAJORDOMUS_BIN` it builds nothing; without it, it builds with cargo into the directory cargo names (the shared build directory) and the hooks judge the tree's own `target/debug/majordomus`.
- The seat's observation of 2026-11-06 17:00Z: after authoring 1.0.0 in `Cargo.toml`, `cargo build` then `scripts/derive` left the executable "stale (older than its sources)", the commit hook and cases 10, 85 and 87b refused, and a second build followed by `scripts/derive-check` held.
- The order (bump, build, derive, build again, derive-check) is the remedy; which writer touched the crate after the first build was not pinned in that run, so `docs/DISTRIBUTION.md`'s Releasing step 1 is not changed here and owes the line once it is.
