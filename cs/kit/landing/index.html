Řídicí dozorová vrstva

Jeden repozitář.Mnoho perspektiv.Jeden zdroj pravdy.

Your AI agents say the work is done. Majordomus makes them prove it. It reads the repository's .ai/ layer into typed models and serves them to the command line, the HTTP API, MCP, the Cockpit and this site.
