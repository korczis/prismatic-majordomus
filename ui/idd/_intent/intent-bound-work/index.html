Intent

What the work is for, what it holds the work to, and whether the evidence says it is met.

intent-bound-work

Work starts bound to the intent it serves, and a worker resumes knowing whether that intent moved

plannedunsatisfied2 of 5 criteria metClose

A task that starts names the work it executes — an issue, an intent, or an exemption the policy declares — and the repository resolves from that, once, which intent and which criteria the task serves; where the policy requires it, a task whose binding is refused does not start. The worker is briefed with what the intent asks of this work, and a handover carries the revision of the intent it was written under, so the next worker is told when the intent, its criteria, its critique or the standing of its evidence changed in between.

What it holds the work to

  • A task stores what it names — an issue id, an intent id, an exemption with its reason — and a revision pin; the intent, its stage, its verdict and its criteria are derived on every read and stored in no task, session or handover
  • The binding is one derivation in the intent engine, answered by one capability; start, context and handover ask it and decide nothing themselves
  • An exemption is a class the policy declares, given with a reason and recorded in the ledger; the absence of an intent is never an exemption by itself
  • A repository whose policy does not ask for binding starts tasks exactly as before
