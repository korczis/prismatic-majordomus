What is this intent for?

What the work is for, what it holds the work to, and whether the evidence says it is met.

the-product-proves-it-pays

Majordomus proves, by measured runs, that it delivers accepted work with less cost, risk and human effort than the same agents without it

plannedunknown0 of 27 criteria metClose

Whether Majordomus is worth adopting is settled by recorded, reproducible measurement, never by a feature count or a forecast: on repositories not built around it and on a corpus of comparable tasks registered and stratified before any run, the same agents with and without Majordomus are compared on accepted results, wall time, human interventions, CI reruns, spend and recovery from injected faults; a newcomer reaches a first verified intent unaided; and the verdict of every test, losses included, is published beside the claim it settles.

What does it hold the work to?

  • No status, verdict or figure is typed: every verdict is computed from committed records against a protocol registered before the first record it counts
  • A protocol edited after registration is a new version; a record carries the digest of its own test's protocol and counts only toward that version, so registering or editing one test never makes another test's records stale
  • A failed or inconclusive test, a lost pair or trial, and an invalid or uncounted record are published in the same row as the claim they bear on, never dropped or folded into a total
  • No public sentence of the claims matrix or the homepage copy states an advantage that a passed proof test does not back
  • A record of a repository whose publication basis is neither a licence nor the owner's dated written consent carries no name, raw commit id, path or free-text observation
  • Nothing about an outside person's run is simulated, edited or inferred; a run the person did not do is not a run
  • No fix decides by the identity of the repository it runs in
  • One calculator: effects, intervals and verdicts come from apps/majordomus-cli/src/economics/stats.rs, and every surface reads one capability
