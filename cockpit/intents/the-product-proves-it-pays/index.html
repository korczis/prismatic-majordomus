Majordomus proves, by measured runs, that it delivers accepted work with less cost, risk and human effort than the same agents without it

intent the-product-proves-it-pays

How far reality is from it

Whether Majordomus is worth adopting is settled by recorded, reproducible measurement, never by a feature count or a forecast: on repositories not built around it and on a corpus of comparable tasks registered and stratified before any run, the same agents with and without Majordomus are compared on accepted results, wall time, human interventions, CI reruns, spend and recovery from injected faults; a newcomer reaches a first verified intent unaided; and the verdict of every test, losses included, is published beside the claim it settles.
