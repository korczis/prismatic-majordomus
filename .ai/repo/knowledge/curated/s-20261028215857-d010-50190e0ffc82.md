# 641b's free disk fell to 0.4-3 GiB twice on 2026-10-28 while several sessions built into the shared build dir: the sweep is skipped while a build holds cargo's lock and lib/rust_bin.sh refuses only below 5 GiB, so clause 7's 35 GiB floor is not held at build start; owed by I3825 and I3959

# Evidence

- Rule `project.the-cooperation-model-is-the-owners-word` clause 7 says no build starts below 35 GiB
  free; its own Verification section records that `lib/rust_bin.sh` still refuses only below 5 GiB
  (`MAJORDOMUS_MIN_FREE_MB`, default 5120) and that the 35 GiB guard is owed by I3825.
- 641b's free disk fell to 0.4-3 GiB twice on 2026-10-28 (the mesh claim `disk`, 07a5ffde p2,
  21:40Z: build-shared 63 GB to 40 GB after the sweep, still under the floor).
- Not every drain is a build. On 2026-10-26 the free disk fell from 44 to 15 GiB in 40 minutes with
  no cargo running: background shell watcher loops (`while :; do ...; sleep N; done`) left by agent
  sessions had grown to 55, 7 and 3 GB of memory over two to three hours, and swap, which lives on the
  boot disk, grew from 4 to 22 GB. `ps` under-reports such a process; `top -o mem` and
  `sysctl vm.swapusage` show it. A floor that counts only builders misses this drain.
- In flight, linked and not fixed here: I3825 (the build refuses below 35 GiB or beside five builders)
  and I3959 (the shared build dir is evicted under cargo's lock while builds run). Raising the
  default here could refuse a CI runner with less than 35 GiB free; I3825 owns that choice.
