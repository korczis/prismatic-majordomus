title = "I4704 — plan.findings derives every open finding from the layer: what is blocked, left out of scope with no follow-up, handed to another repository, not run, partial or drifting, each with its locator"
description = "A read-only capability plan.findings (CLI `majordomus plan findings [--json] [--live]`, MCP majordomus_plan_findings, GET /api/v1/plan/findings) answers one list of findings computed from the layer and written by nobody. Each finding has `kind`, `text`, `path`, `line`, `record`, `follow_up` (the open issue ids that take it over, empty when none does) and `label`. The label is OBSERVED when a record states the open status itself, and DERIVED when the answer computed it from several records. Built-in kinds: `blocked` (an open issue with a dependency that is not done, naming the blocker), `out-of-scope` (a non_scope entry of a DONE issue that no open issue cites), `not-run` and `partial` (an evidence result or a task outcome carrying a status the policy declares open, such as NOT RUN, PARTIAL or FAIL), `outbound` (an intent, critique or proposal addressed to another repository with no recorded answer), and `drift` (plan.drift's non-advisory findings, read only with `--live`, since they depend on the moment). A repository adds its own vocabulary under a policy key `findings:` (open statuses, Markdown table columns, front-matter fields and an in-document marker). The output carries the same fields as a repository-local generator that an adopter already runs, so that generator can become a thin projection of this capability. A derived page under docs/ is regenerated by the existing derive step and checked fresh by it."
