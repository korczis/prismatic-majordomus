pub fn read_generated_schema_dir(
    dir: &Path,

Expand description

Every <name>.schema.json directly under a directory, parsed, keyed by that <name> and sorted by it. A directory that does not exist yields nothing.

This is the reader for <share>/schemas/generated/, whose files are the contracts of the generated documents rather than of the layer’s kinds: they answer to the document id they pin in properties.schema.const, not to a <vendor>/<name> path, so the shape read_schema_dir insists on does not apply to them.
