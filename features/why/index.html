The failure modes it answers are objects, not marketing pages

Every operational moment the tool exists for is one file with the signals that identify it and the mechanisms that answer it, and a reader's own symptoms resolve to a diagnosis by counting, not by inference.

status
✓feature status: stable
domain
Surfaces

What it does

majordomus why lists the moments, narrowed by any facet the catalogue reports; why diagnose takes the signals a reader recognises and answers with the areas they weigh towards and the mechanisms that answer them, each recommendation carrying the moments that produced it; why validate refuses a reference that resolves to nothing, offering the nearest candidate.

What it does not do

A moment that would be fixed by a better model does not belong in the catalogue. There is no weighting and no percentage in a diagnosis, because there is no model behind one.

How it works

The capability modules of the executable this feature names, each with the capabilities it composes. The interfaces each one reaches are listed under Interfaces below: they are projections of one declaration, not separate registrations.

Why why behaviorally verified

The operational failure modes this tool is a response to: the moments a reader recognises, the audiences that recognise them, the areas they fall under, and what a reader's own symptoms imply. Every entry is a file under the layer's why section; nothing here holds a list, and a moment added there is answered by all of these without a registration anywhere.

Using it

From the executable's command line

The synopsis of each, as the executable's own help prints it.

  • majordomus why list [OPTIONS]

    Every operational moment, narrowed by any facet the catalogue reports

  • majordomus why show [OPTIONS] <ID>

    One moment in full, with every relation derived from its metadata

  • majordomus why audiences [OPTIONS]

    Every audience, with the moments that name it

  • majordomus why areas [OPTIONS]

    Every operational area, with the moments that fall under it

  • majordomus why diagnose [OPTIONS]

    What the symptoms you recognise imply: the areas they weigh towards and the mechanisms that answer them

  • majordomus why validate [OPTIONS]

    Every finding over the catalogue; exit 10 when any is an error
