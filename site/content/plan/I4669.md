title = "I4669 — An owner's answer is a typed, committed record with its provenance, so a session that receives it relayed cites it by id and a validator proves where it came from"
description = "An owner-answer record kind (majordomus.owner-answer/v1) holds one question put to the repository's owner and the answer. Its fields are an id, the channel it was asked through (a provider's question tool, a terminal prompt, a forge comment), the asking session's id, the time it was asked and the time it was answered, the question and the answer verbatim, the options offered when the channel offered any, and the sessions it was relayed through (relayed_by, relayed_to, each with its time). `majordomus answer record` writes one record from the asking session. The session id and the times are computed, never typed. A record whose text a policy marks private is committed as its digest, with the text kept under .ai/local. `majordomus answer relay <id> --to <session>` appends one relay hop. `majordomus answer show <id>` prints it for the receiving session to read. A rule, decision, issue or ADR cites the answer as `owner-answer:<id>` and does not retype it. A validator, run by doctor and by plan validate, refuses a citation that does not resolve. It also refuses a quoted answer that differs from the record's verbatim text, and a relay hop whose receiving session is not one the hop's sender could reach."
