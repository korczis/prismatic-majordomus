# A merge of master into a pull request branch is refused by the commit hook in two alternating ways: scripts/derive's merge-aware stage A writes a changelog that generate --check, judging from the old head's history, calls stale; and majordomus generate alone leaves site/data stale. What committed it (#2414, 2026-11-01): majordomus generate, then scripts/generate-site-data, then majordomus generate again, commit, then scripts/derive on the committed merge and a second commit. Owned by I4513 (pull request 2759).

Observed as workaround about gate:pages in episode s-20261101044146-a241.

# Evidence

Reproduced on #2414 (2026-11-01): the commit of a merge of master aca6f182b41 was refused once for a stale changelog after scripts/derive and once for stale site data after majordomus generate alone.
