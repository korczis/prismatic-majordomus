description = "`majordomus monitor run` waits on one workflow run, named by id or by branch and head SHA as `monitor steps` takes it, until a verdict is reached. Every job of the run's latest attempt must conclude success (exit 0). Any job that concludes anything else makes the wait red at once (exit 10), including a job that starts after the others have passed. The exits 11 (timeout) and 12 (unreadable) are #3067's. With `--gates <tier>` the jobs judged are those of the CI model (.ai/repo/ci/gates.yaml) whose gates the tier's lanes select. On-demand gates are excluded. A modelled job absent from a finished run is red, and a job the model does not name is reported but does not gate. Without a model, `--gates` is refused (12) and names `majordomus init --extend --ci`. `monitor steps` keeps its range verdict, but its green output says that the range is green and the run is not judged. It names the run's jobs still pending and carries `run_concluded` in JSON, so the exit 0 of a range is never read as permission to land."
