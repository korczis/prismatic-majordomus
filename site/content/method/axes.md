{% raw %}
Ask what a piece of AI-assisted work *is* and four different answers get folded into one
field, usually called `model`. They are not the same question, they do not change at the same
rate, and collapsing them is how a repository ends up with a vendor's name compiled into its
build.

They separate cleanly.
{% endraw %}
