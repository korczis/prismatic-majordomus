## 167. Work moves through seats on the mesh, lands in combined tops, and every checkout is kept current

### Context

ADR 0101 made integration one typed subsystem that lands one pull request per run of the
required check, and listed batches among the alternatives it rejected: "They remain a
person's decision, not the executor's." ADR 0155 recorded the owner spending the branch
protection for a named lander, one landing at a time, pinned to a judged head.

By the morning of 2026-10-16 more than a hundred pull requests were open (107 at 09:10Z),
nine or more sessions worked the repository at once on one machine, and three defects of the
practice were measured rather than argued:

<div class="overflow-x-auto" tabindex="0">

| what happened | what it cost |
|---|---|
| each landing put every other open head behind master again | a merge of master, a derive and a run of about an hour per pull request, so the queue grew faster than one lander could drain it |
| the primary checkout stood 779 commits behind master | its server answered the plan from that tree, and a session cancelled a sweep of fourteen issues after finding thirteen of them already done on master |
| releases waited for milestones | the published site and binaries named a version that master had long passed |

</div>


The owner answered the sessions that morning. The words, as written to them, in Czech, with
an English reading beside each:

- "strasne rychle odbavovat milestones/issues/waves, deploy+bump+vv often, from now always,
  use majordomus mesh" (clear milestones, issues and waves very fast; deploy, bump and the
  rest often, from now on always; use the Majordomus mesh).
- "rozdelte si to, spolupracujte a at to odsejpa" (split the work between you, cooperate,
  and make it move).
- Asked whether to land combined tops or one pull request at a time: combined tops; and a
  standing approval for pinned admin merges, `gh pr merge <n> --admin --merge
  --match-head-commit <sha>`, only when that exact head's `ci` concluded success and the head
  contains the current `origin/master`.
- "moje rozhodnuti je vzdy vse updatovat, rebasovat, force-forward, whatever helps, document
  it, describe it (gh pages / official pages / ui) a pokracuj s puvodnim planem" (my decision
  is always to update everything, rebase, force forward, whatever helps; document it,
  describe it on the published site and in the UI, and continue with the original plan).
- "vse derived, inferred, generic, generated, dynamic, enforced for future" (everything
  derived, inferred, generic, generated, dynamic, and enforced from now on).

### Decision

The practice in force is rule `project.the-cooperation-model-is-the-owners-word`. It is the
one record of it: the integration, mesh and release documents point at the rule rather than
restating it, the site renders the rule and this decision from these files, and the Mesh
screen of the UI (`/ui/mesh`) draws who holds each seat from the live claims of the mesh,
never from a list of names.

1. **Seats are mesh claims.** A seat is held by holding its exclusive claim on the mesh, and
   nothing else makes a session a seat. The landing seat holds
   `campaign/<campaign>/landing` while it lands; the release seat holds `release/seat`; a
   repair lane holds `refs/heads/<branch>` for each branch it brings current; a worker
   holds the paths of its one issue. Because the seat is the claim, it moves when the claim
   moves, a crashed holder's seat expires with its heartbeat, and the UI reads it from
   `mesh.state` rather than from anybody's memory.
2. **Combined tops.** The landing seat may land many pull requests in one run of the
   required check: master plus the heads of pull requests that conflict with nothing in
   authored paths (decided by a chain of `git merge-tree`), derived files regenerated once
   after the merge commit, one validation run judging every member, a red member dropped and
   fixed on its own branch, and one pinned merge landing the top, after which the forge marks
   every contained pull request merged. A release's bump may ride inside a top.
3. **Always current.** A stale checkout or branch is brought current without asking anyone,
   after what it holds is preserved: `git stash create` and `git update-ref
   refs/rescue/<name>` for uncommitted work, a patch under `<repository>-rescue/` for
   anything else. A checkout or a branch nobody else has fetched may be reset, rebased or
   fast-forwarded; a pushed branch is brought current by merging master into it and pushing
   a fast-forward.
4. **Release often.** The release seat cuts a release whenever master holds landed work
   past the last tag, not once per milestone, and verifies what the site serves afterwards.

#### What this amends

ADR 0101's executor is unchanged: `majordomus prs drain` still lands one ready pull request
at a time, never passes `--admin`, and holds its lease while it acts. What changes is the
lander's own act. ADR 0101 left batches to a person; the owner has made that decision, so a
combined top is no longer a hand-built exception but the landing seat's ordinary unit of
work. ADR 0155's clause "one landing at a time" now reads: one landing seat at a time, and a
landing may carry a whole top.

#### What still holds

- Every decision is taken against the current `origin/master` and the head SHAs it names,
  and is acted on only while they still hold; `--match-head-commit` refuses a head that
  moved.
- `--admin` is passed only pinned to a head whose `ci` concluded success and which contains
  the current master: the protection is spent on a judged tree, never to skip a check.
- No check, hook or gate is disabled, skipped or edited to land; `--no-verify` is never used.
- A release is never expedited: a tag is pushed only on a commit whose `ci` succeeded
  (`scripts/ci/release-verdict`).
- No pushed branch is force-pushed. The owner's "force-forward" is read as "bring it
  forward by whatever helps"; on a branch other sessions and the forge read, what helps is a
  merge, because a rewritten remote head cannot be recovered by the rescue ref of the
  session that rewrote it.
- Evidence is recorded for each decision: the master and head a top was built from, its
  members, the run that judged it, and the commit production serves after it.

### Consequences

Throughput is no longer bounded by one pull request per run: a top of twenty members costs one
run. A top's red is attributed by its jobs to a member, which is dropped and fixed on its own
branch, so the attribution ADR 0101 protected survives at the cost of one more run per red
member.

Combined tops are still assembled by the landing seat's own procedure, not by a capability of
the executor; the typed `prs compose` that would record a top's members as a manifest never
landed. Until it does, the record of a top is its pull request: its body names the members
and their heads, and its merge commit's parents are master and the top.

Three enforcement points are carried by other workers and are named by the rule as they land:
a new worktree starts from the remote trunk rather than a stale local master; `start` makes
sure the checkout's server runs before a task begins; and a checkout that has fallen behind is
detected and brought current.

### Alternatives rejected

- **Keep one pull request per run.** Measured on 2026-10-15 and 16: the queue outgrew it.
- **Name the seats in a document.** A list of sessions is stale the moment one reconnects;
  the claim is already the fact, so the UI reads the claim.
- **Force-push stale branches.** It is fast for the session that does it and destroys work
  another session pushed in the meantime; a merge of master costs one commit.

### Amendment, 2026-10-20: a worker lands its own green head

At about 23:13 UTC on 2026-10-20 the owner relaxed the model: "hlavne prosim tebe reflektuj
zmekceni pravidel, vse updatuj, vsem dej vedet" ("above all, reflect the relaxation of the
rules, update everything, let everyone know"), choosing every relaxation offered (build and disk
limits, landing and merge, the sessions' permissions, coordination formalities). The seats read
it as: a worker lands its own green pull request through the guarded lander's queue, pinned
exactly as a top is pinned; at most five builders on one machine and none below 35 GiB free;
cleanup that loses nothing is done without asking and announced; ids, paths and seats are still
claimed first. Rule `project.the-cooperation-model-is-the-owners-word` version 2 records it
(I3824); the landing seat keeps combined tops for heads that must ship together. What may reach
master is unchanged: a head whose `ci` succeeded and which contains the current master.
